The Band

OneOkRock

A Japanese rock band with a global sound — mixing post-hardcore roots with arena-ready hooks.

Formation

OneOkRock formed in 2005 in Tokyo, originally emerging from the local live house scene. From early emo and post-hardcore influences, the band steadily expanded their range, adding pop-rock sheen and, later, English-language lyrics aimed at international audiences.

Sound

Known for melodic vocal hooks, driving rhythms, and emotional lyrics, OneOkRock's catalogue spans anthemic rock ("The Beginning"), piano-driven ballads ("I was King"), and arena-scale choruses ("Stand Out Fit In").

Global reach

Through relentless touring and soundtrack appearances, the band built a worldwide following across Japan, North America, South America, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
