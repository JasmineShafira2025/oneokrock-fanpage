FAQ

Is this site official?

No — it's a fan-run hub. For official news, check the band's verified channels.

Can I submit photos?

Yes, use the form and select "Photo submission". Credit goes to the photographer.

How are tour dates verified?

We cross-check with official announcements and venue listings. Always confirm with the venue before traveling.

Can I help build the site?

Absolutely — reach out via the form. We're especially looking for translators and regional tour-date contributors.
